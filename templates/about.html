How to play:

Hit the play button and listen to the mystery song! You get three attempts to guess the title, artist, and year that the song came out. If you guess one of the categories correctly, you get some points! If you miss all three categories on each of the attempts, you lose.

Scoring guide:

If you lose the round, the score is 0. Otherwise, the score is calculated by how many categories you guess, and which attempt you guess each category correct on!

Multipliers:

We calculate the score using multipliers that change with each attempt you guess.

Title score multipliers:

  • Attempt 1 - 1.25
  • Attempt 2 - 1.15
  • Attempt 3 - 1.1

Artist score multipliers:

  • Attempt 1 - 1.2
  • Attempt 2 - 1.1
  • Attempt 3 - 1.05

Year score multipliers:

  • Attempt 1 - 1.5
  • Attempt 2 - 1.4
  • Attempt 3 - 1.3
We understand that knowing the exact year that a song came out is hard to guess. That's why we've decided to make the multipliers for that category much higher, so that those hardcore fans can get some high scores. There's also a super secret bonus multiplier that you get for guessing all three categories!
